# Verification Protocol & Quality Checklist

Before delivering any presentation slide deck or quotation, verify all content against this 5-point checklist:

---

## 1. Mathematical Rigour & Financial Integrity
- [ ] **Line Items:** Every item total strictly equals `quantity × unit_price` or `length/area × unit_price`.
- [ ] **Section / Room Subtotals:** Each section subtotal strictly equals the exact arithmetic sum of its constituent items.
- [ ] **Grand Total:** The grand total on the summary slide strictly equals the exact sum of all section subtotals (plus tax where specified).
- [ ] **Zero Hallucination:** No hand-estimated or fabricated prices. All calculations are verified step-by-step.

## 2. Localization & Typography
- [ ] **Currency & Number Formatting:** Formatted per local conventions (e.g. `$4,200.00` / `4.200.000đ`).
- [ ] **Decimal Units:** Decimals use regional standards (`2.5 m`, `3.8 m²` or `2,5 md`, `3,8 m²`).
- [ ] **Placeholders:** All placeholder tags (`[__] days`, `[__] USD/VND`) are replaced or clearly resolved.
- [ ] **Word Budget:** Copy remains concise, at most ~90 words per slide to eliminate vertical overflow.

## 3. Visual Structure & Editorial Hierarchy
- [ ] **Slide Canvas:** Strict 16:9 canvas (1280×720 px, `@page { size: 1280px 720px; margin: 0; }`).
- [ ] **Information Hierarchy:** Each slide has a distinct eyebrow/category, main title, structured content, and layout/art direction notes.
- [ ] **Editorial Rhythm:** Slides alternate between photo-led narrative and data/table-led layouts.

## 4. Art Direction & Image Prompts
- [ ] **Structured Prompts:** Every image prompt includes subject, setting, materials, lighting, framing, and aspect ratio.
- [ ] **Style Anchor:** Consistent style anchor applied across all visual prompts (e.g. *Soft natural afternoon daylight, warm cream plaster walls, light oak wood and natural linen, muted earthy palette, photorealistic architectural photography, calm and minimal, clean composition, no text, no logos*).
- [ ] **Zero Text in Images:** Never request AI image generators to draw text, prices, or numbers inside images.

## 5. Completeness & Scope
- [ ] **Scope Demarcation:** In quotation decks, explicitly state what is included and excluded (structural works, MEP, appliances, loose décor).
- [ ] **Terms & Conditions:** Payment schedule (e.g., 3 installments: 40% - 40% - 20%), validity period, and warranty terms are clearly stated.
